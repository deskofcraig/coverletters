About the job
Our team is growing the design capability within Risk Management Group and focuses on improving the digital experience across internal platforms. We create simple, user-centred solutions that support risk, compliance and transformation initiatives across the organisation.

We are a global financial services group operating in 30 markets with 57 years of unbroken profitability. At Macquarie, you’re empowered to shape a career that is fulfilling and creates value. You will bring your insights and expertise to the task at hand and feel supported as you make your own kind of impact for a better future.

 What role will you play? 

In this role you will lead the design of complex digital products from discovery through to delivery. You will work closely with business stakeholders, engineers, business analysts and designers to create intuitive and scalable experiences, using research and user insights to shape design decisions that support both user and business outcomes.

What You Offer

5+ years’ of experience in UX/UI or Product Design, ideally within financial services, risk, compliance or internal platforms.
Strong experience delivering end-to-end product design, including user research, journey mapping, information architecture, interaction design, prototyping, usability testing and visual design.
Experience working in agile teams and collaborating with a range of business and technology stakeholders.
Proficiency in Figma, with knowledge of design systems and accessibility standards.
Strong communication and stakeholder engagement skills, with the ability to balance user needs, business goals and technical requirements.

We love hearing from anyone inspired to build a better future with us, if you're excited about the role or working at Macquarie we encourage you to apply.

Our Risk Management Group works as an independent, and centralised function, responsible for independent and objective review and challenge, oversight, monitoring and reporting in relation to Macquarie’s material risks.
