```
Hello to the Flight Centre team 👋

I am excited to apply for your UX/Designer role focusing on Design System Operations. Design Systems and Component Libraries are a passionate field of design and experience for me.  A thoughtful and well-managed design system can create and continue to create consistent, relevant, trustworthy, and iconic experiences for customers, users , and visitors. It does not end there, as engineers and designers needing access to the design system when creating, designing and building interfaces have the exact building to maintain the high level of quality and experience when delivering completed work.

I have industry practiced knowledge in design system knowledge from initial stages of concepting needs to lifecycle management and scalability, I have had first hand experience with guideline,  asset, token, and component creation through using tools such as Figma, Supernova, Storybook and many more. I have collaborated and helped manage existing design systems at Octopus Deploy as well as work with stakeholders and design team members to establish new components and guidelines as product and user needs have required them. The design system was managed in a federated method, which we have set up to automate challenges made in Figma, Supernova, and Storybook to communicate and push changes made to make sure all systems and tools were consistent, up-to-date, and available. When new work was required, I would branch the relevant files to freely work on the creation of new assets, regardless of what tools were being updated.  


I believe a good design system is only as good as its documentation. I have written guidelines, and usage guidance and state variation rules on management software like Supernova to allow for discoverable and easily understood component, layout, and interactions usages and requirements. I have run workshops and exploratory sessions to understand the requirements and need of a new or existing component with stakeholders, designers and engineers to understand are components needs and use cases. This practice allows for consistency and requirements of a component being understood to make sure it is something that will help solve a problem, whether it is formalising a snowflake component, an interaction method for users, or new experience feature. 

In multiple workplaces I have created and managed design systems for clients and in-house requirements. At Servian/Cognizant, I created and managed a design system for a national health services client that was redeveloping from the ground up their internal systems for managing pathology referrals, collections, couriers & transportation, testing, and results. This was a large piece of work that we needed to ideate, design, and develop component and design rules for interfaces relating to these jobs. Not only where the internal staff and contractor were considered when developing this design system but also the patients being referred, receiving their results, and any actions that they were required to do.

These experiences have meant I have had to identify and implement operational improvements to the design systems and components I have worked on. I have worked with engineers, designers, stakeholders, and products to make sure changes, additions, and future maintenance is organised and considered. This process was done at CoreLogic when I introduced design systems and components libraries to the head of marketing and the marketing when they were redeveloping their marketing website. I was sure to communicate with all required parties to make sure a documented and functional design system and component linked to the organisation's branding guidelines was delivered ahead of schedule. This was a successfully delivered project as the design system was followed when I built key flows and screens for the website in Figma and its continued use  by marketing post project work. Post handover, I monitored the system through surveys and metrics to understand knowledge, usage and pain points to communicate to organise improvements, reporting, and increase engagement with marketing, sales and product teams. 


I have always run my work through  WCAG compliance and guidelines to meet high levels of accessibility and best practices. I developed service design interfaces to Queensland Police. Due to them being a public government organisation, accessibility guidelines are very strict and need to meet high standards beyond best practice. This experience has led to me making sure that “best practice” is only the benchmark in accessibility standards and can go further. To achieve this, extensive auditing and testing was completed to ensure all WCAG and internal accessibility standards and requirements were met.

I have 10+ years experience in product and user experience design and have created, developed, and managed 5 design systems across 5 organisations, products, and clients. I am extremely proficient with design software such Figma, and design system tools such as Supernova and Storybook. I have a deep understanding of design system governance and scalability, as well as the need for documenting for interaction flows, usage rules, and accessibility standards. I have industry practiced knowledge of HTML, CSS, Javascript, and JQuery in helping to build components. I use AI tools like Claude to help develop and design assets to improve and increase speed in  workflows. I conduct benchmarking surveys for metrics and analytics to improve design system usage, adoption, and performance. 

I would love to talk more about this role and what my experience and knowledge can bring to Flight Center to help drive development, consistently and trust with your community of users.

I hope to hear from you soon,
Craig Johnson
```
