About the job
Your new company
An established organisation is seeking an experienced UI/UX Designer / Business Analyst to support the delivery of several concurrent transformation initiatives. Your new role
As a UI/UX Designer / BA, you will act as a subject matter expert across three concurrent projects, balancing user-centred design with business analysis activities. You will work closely with business stakeholders, project teams and end users to define requirements, create engaging user experiences and ensure successful project outcomes within tight delivery timeframes.

Key responsibilities include:
Facilitating workshops and engaging stakeholders to gather and validate requirements
Creating user journeys, wireframes, prototypes and design artefacts using Figma
Translating business requirements into intuitive and user-friendly solutions
Working across multiple projects simultaneously while managing competing priorities
Collaborating with project teams to deliver outcomes within challenging deadlines
Providing UI/UX expertise and recommendations to support project delivery
Conducting user research and incorporating feedback into design improvements
Supporting solution design and business process improvement initiatives

What you'll need to succeedTo be successful in this role, you will bring:
Proven experience as a UI/UX Designer, with Business Analysis experience highly regarded
Advanced experience using Figma to create wireframes, prototypes and user-centric designs
Strong stakeholder engagement, workshop facilitation and requirements gathering skills
Experience working across multiple projects in fast-paced delivery environments
The ability to manage competing priorities and tight deadlines
Strong communication and presentation skills
Experience translating business requirements into effective customer-focused solutions
A proactive, consultative approach with the ability to operate as a trusted SME

Previous consulting experience with organisations such as Accenture, Deloitte, Capgemini, EY, PwC or similar consulting environments will be highly regarded.
