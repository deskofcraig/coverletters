Senior Product Designer [AQ-22679]

About the job
Aquent is partnering with a leading telecommunications provider, a powerhouse in connecting businesses across the nation, to find exceptional talent. This is a unique opportunity to make a significant impact, driving the evolution of critical digital experiences that empower businesses to thrive. You will be at the forefront of innovation, directly influencing the usability and functionality of a vital B2B self-service platform that underpins how medium-sized businesses manage their services and operations. Your work will directly translate strategic vision into seamless, intuitive digital solutions, shaping the future of business interaction with essential services.

About The Opportunity

We are seeking a highly driven and delivery-focused Senior Product Designer to join a high-impact squad. This team is dedicated to redesigning a core B2B self-service platform, a crucial portal utilized by medium-sized businesses for managing orders, requests, billing, and various services. In this pivotal role, you will act as the squad’s execution lead, working in close collaboration with a Strategic UX Lead who sets the macro-strategy. Your expertise will be essential in translating these strategic frameworks into pixel-perfect, highly functional web UI/UX, ensuring an intuitive and efficient user experience.

Key Responsibilities

Own the tactical end-to-end interface design for critical areas, including the Home Page and the Products & Services portal.
Build and meticulously maintain scalable UI components, interaction patterns, state mappings, and developer-ready assets within Figma.
Partner closely with Product, Engineering, and Strategic UX teams to transform complex business logic into intuitive and seamless web flows.
Produce clear, comprehensive design specifications, ensuring a smooth and efficient handover process for developers.

What We’re Looking For

A balanced and robust UI/UX skillset, demonstrating strong visual design (UI) foundations complemented by pragmatic user flow and interaction design capabilities.
Proven experience in designing complex enterprise web platforms, intricate portals, or data-dense dashboards, with a strong B2B focus.
An obsessive attention to craft and precision, particularly concerning edge cases, system states, design systems, and component architecture in Figma.
A squad-first mindset, characterized by a calm, dependable, and low-ego approach. You thrive on execution, foster strong collaboration, and consistently deliver solid output within an agile team environment.
