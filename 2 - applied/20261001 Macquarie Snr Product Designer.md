# Job description

```
Help us define and deliver our experience vision across the platforms that power Macquarie's business-to-business banking customers. Working within the Research and Design team, reporting to the Design Lead, you will play a pivotal role in ensuring that Macquarie creates exceptional experiences for the businesses and customers we serve, across desktop and mobile.

We are a global financial services group operating in 30 markets with 57 years of unbroken profitability. At Macquarie, you’re empowered to shape a career that is fulfilling and creates value. You will bring your insights and expertise to the task at hand and feel supported as you make your own kind of impact for a better future.

What role will you play?

As an instrumental player, you'll be pivotal in shaping and advancing our design standards. Your role involves crafting innovative and user-friendly UX/UI solutions that address complex challenges while aligning with our vision and organisational goals. As a senior member in our team, you'll drive user-centered design activities, pushing the boundaries of conventional thinking and forging new methodologies to navigate intricate design issues — including designing AI-native, working directly in tools like Claude to move fast from concept to high-
fidelity output. Your expertise will also be key in designing and leading workshops that foster ideation and iteration, whether working with small teams or large groups, and regardless of whether the collaboration is remote or in-person.

In this collaborative environment, you'll work alongside Business Analysts, Engineers, Product Managers, Researchers, and Designers to tackle problems and strategically prioritise design activities and research. You'll stand as a champion for our customers, translating research findings into compelling narratives and sharing them with diverse audiences through detailed reports and persuasive presentations. You'll also mentor and support less experienced designers in the team, helping to grow their craft and connect their work to the broader practice, and contribute to our shared design system, patterns and accessibility standards. 

What you offer

5+ years working as a UX and UI Designer or Product Designer, including experience conducting UX research
Formal education in a relevant field such as Design, Research, Human Computer-Interaction, or similar
Shipped multiple digital products and understand what it takes to create exceptional customer experiences
Excellent portfolio showcasing in-depth knowledge and experience of UX and UI for mobile, desktop and web-based applications. A focus on native mobile is highly regarded
End-to-end experience of managing design activities across the user-centered design process including strong design research skills, iterative design and prototyping and facilitation of usability testing 
AI-native approach to design, comfortable creating high-quality artefacts such as personas, journey maps, task models, wireframes and interactive prototypes directly through AI tools, with minimal reliance on traditional design software 
Experience with AI prompting for design workflows, preferably with Claude Comfortable working in an Agile environment and resilience in the face of shifting priorities
Boundless curiosity about how people use technology and how to create great experiences
Highly regarded: experience with strata or property-related processes, or working in a business-to- business / customer relationship environment

We love hearing from anyone inspired to build a better future with us, if you're excited about the role or working at Macquarie we encourage you to apply.

Hello Macquarie GTS Design Team 👋,

I'm applying for the Senior Product Designer role on the business-to-business banking platforms. Most of the last seven years of my work has been self-service purchasing, billing and payment flows, where a business customer has to decide, pay and manage something without picking up the phone. That's what this role is about.

At Octopus Deploy I led two joint outcomes over two years. The first was self-service purchasing and subscription management for the Starter and Professional tiers. Before it, customers went back and forth with sales reps and technical support just to land on the right plan. I ran the customer interviews and workshops myself and tested the a la carte selection (projects, tenants, machines) using a paradigm similar to how people purchase a pair of shoes online. Starter tier purchases rose 25% and 10 customers bought Professional tier subscriptions without a sales conversation in the first month. Those numbers look small, but it's a niche DevOps product sold mostly as enterprise B2B, and sales soon started announcing enterprise wins more regularly due to not having to focus on potentially smaller accounts. The second outcome was Policies and Compliance, including pre-runtime compliance testing that catches a deployment that would break before it runs. Catching a costly failure early in a high-stakes process is the same instinct I'd bring to banking.

Money movement runs through my earlier roles too. At Amber Labs I designed one payment experience that adapted across jurisdictions and payment methods, and the product was nominated for a Finnies Award. My time at Amber was spent working towards “making bitcoin easy” and building easy and trustworthy purchasing and selling, sending and receiving, and funding and withdrawing. These were all handled through native mobile applications making the experience as easy and convenient as possible for our users.

At CoreLogic I owned UX for the billing portal, which won a 2021 Pinnacle Award, and designed a single sign-on experience across the whole product suite. Support tickets about multiple passwords and accounts dropped steadily after launch. I haven't designed for strata managers directly, but I spent two years inside the RP Data, ValueEx and Cordell teams building for property and construction data products, so property workflows aren't new to me. I also spent 11 years as an insurance broker before moving into design, so I know what a commercial customer is dealing with when something goes wrong.

At Octopus Deploy, I work with the Foundations team to develop and contribute assets for their design system and components that were stored across Supernova, Figma, Storybook.
