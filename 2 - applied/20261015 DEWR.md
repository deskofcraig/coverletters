About the job
APS Level 6
Canberra, ACT; Melbourne, VIC; Sydney, NSW; Brisbane, QLD.

About The Department Of Employment And Workplace Relations

We are committed to empowering futures, ensuring rights and enriching lives. We support people to have safe, secure and well-paid work with the skills for a sustainable future as we ourselves strive to be a model employer.

We provide the framework for fair and productive relationships between employers and employees. We also oversee skills development and training initiatives to support those entering the workforce or re-training to take advantage of emerging employment opportunities.

With us, your work directly contributes to shaping the employment landscape. Whether you are engaging in policy development, program implementation, or direct support services, your efforts will have a tangible impact on job seekers, employees and the economy by giving them a framework to build a future anchored and realised through employment.

It is our ambition to do things differently, to create the ‘department of great jobs’– where our people reflect our purpose.

The Role

As a User Experience and User Interface (UX/UI) Designer, you will work with, or as part of, one or multiple delivery teams responsible for building enterprise class systems for the department.

In a predominantly Microsoft and Azure technology environment, you will work in an agile based environment with Service Designers, Digital Accessibility Officers, Business and System Analysts, Software Designers and Developers, SCRUM Teams, Product Owners, Product Managers, and System Users to advocate for, design, review, and assess User Experience outcomes.

You will understand digital diversity and have a passion for User Experience with the necessary people skills to understand user needs and the relevant technical skills to design and prototype user interfaces in line with user needs and business objectives.

The successful applicant will receive the necessary training, mentoring, and guidance to perform the key responsibilities of the role and to advance their careers in the Australian Public Service (APS).

Duties and Responsibilities

Responsibilities of the APS6 Level may include:

Work under limited supervision
Participate in agile ceremonies
Participate in design, planning, and estimation sessions
Conduct user-centred UX/UI design
Analyse information to develop user-centred, user interface designs including wireframes, low-fidelity and high-fidelity prototypes
Work closely with stakeholders and subject matter experts while developing and refining designs to ensure designs meet business objectives
Facilitate stakeholder workshops
Provide design guidance and solutions to delivery teams throughout project and sustainment life cycles
Monitor the team’s workload and delegate tasks as appropriate
If required, travel within Australia to undertake workshops with stakeholders
