Own the digital journey from discovering a club to becoming a member. question 1:
How much of this experience has been with eCommerce, subscription or membership products?

response 1:

I have redesigned payment flows and interfaces for customers that they can trust and have confidence that their purchasing is successful as well as being confident that their banking information is secure throughout. Once they are a customer, I have designed and managed portals for the life of the customer helping them manage, maintain, and understand their purchases and subscriptions. 

I have handled end-to-end customer lifecycle journeys within a product, service and subscription from discovery, to testing, to design, to development, to delivery. 

question 2:
Tell us about a digital experience or ecommerce journey you have redesigned to better communicate a product’s value and improve conversion. 

How did you approach communicating multiple benefits, value adds and pricing, and what impact did your work have?* 

response 2:
While at Octopus Deploy, I led the project to introduce self-service purchasing and subscription management for their Starter and Professional tier products. These tiers are available to customers leaving a free trial period. Both tiers offer the same product but with differences in the experience and capabilities. Most notably, the Professional tier can have more resources attached to the subscription, expanding its potential functionality. When a customer is finishing a free trial and looking to start a subscription for the product, what information are they looking for? What can we tell them based on their usage, resources, team size, and telemetry? I looked into the scenarios that lead customers from leaving, upgrading to Starter or Professional, or beginning an enterprise sales journey. Multiple opportunities were presented to help guide the team towards key outcomes.

One of the outcomes I was tasked for this project  was ”how can we help customers understand and purchase a customisable Professional subscription that met their needs”. This had two goals; increase self-service purchasing  and confidence in decisions, plus help give sales team members more time towards prospective enterprise customers. Product subscriptions at Octopus Deploy have multiple resources and the quantities of these resources can depend on the needs of the customer. I set out to know what parts were required and what constraints we needed to follow. Another question was, “when should the sales team be notified” if a customer would benefit more from an enterprise tier package or higher than expected resources. I worked with the marketing team to redevelop their pricing and tier comparison guides to help display the key differences and opportunities of each of the 3 tiers of the product. This redevelopment led to a clearer understanding of what each tier was offering and what may be the best option for our customers' business needs. 

The Starter tier is a static subscription that can be upgraded to higher tiers but with fixed resources. The Professional tier had to be flow fluid with more options for customisation. I researched and analysed other multiple option customisation purchasing methods and how customers interact with them to purchase exactly what they require. The interface and purchasing system needed to support al-a-carte purchasing as well as offering support for customers wishing to go outside the bounds of the available resources. Individual resource quantities were capped at 100 and grouped into lots of 10 for purchasing. This was consulted with product leads, technical sales support and sales members to make sure the self-servicing experience can be completed by a wide range of small to medium business customers. If higher quantities of a resource were required, a selection option was made available to help highlight the potential customers' need for further sales conversation. This would be a single click to start and conversation with sales. Sales would also be informed of the potential customer and their current purchasing journey through sales and tracking applications like Amplitude and Salesforce. The interface through progressive stage of testing with marketing, sales, stakeholders and a closed community of users lead towards a paradigm similar to purchasing footwear online where you require size, width and colour as basic information to achieve a perfect fit. Our Professional tier product has 5 different resources that can have quantities associated with them which need to be considered in the proposed design solution for customers purchasing a subscription.

Once launched, self-serviced purchasing saw a raise with new and free trial customers continuing subscriptions. Starter tier purchases saw a 25% increase in the first month due the redeveloped comparison charts, and 10 Professional tier self-service purchases were completed in the first month from launch. As a result of Professional tier self servicing being launched and not having to spend as much time on small businesses, enterprise sale wins were announced more regularly by the sales team. These numbers may seem low, but Octopus Deploy is a niche technical heavy developer operations product that is more valuable as an enterprise product used by Fortune 500 software and product companies.
