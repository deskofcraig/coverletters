```

Karmo

Product Manager

Karmo is evolving how people access private transportation. We’ve moved past the "new-age novelty" phase and are now building the sophisticated, multi-user platforms that define car subscription in Australia. We are looking for a Product Manager who doesn't just "ship and forget," but someone who views their product as a living system that requires constant tuning and curiosity.

This role owns the products and platforms Karmo runs on from the inside, the systems the wider business relies on every day. In this role you build for the people beside you: the operators, fellow PMs, Heads and Directors whose teams live in your product, lifting how the whole business serves its end customers. You win through influence, not distance, and the bar for how you carry yourself with senior stakeholders is higher.

🚀 What You’ll Be Doing

Own the Full Lifecycle: Lead products from initial discovery and validation through to build, release, and ongoing optimisation in production.
Lead Discovery & Truth-Seeking: Conduct discovery sessions and user research, poking holes in assumptions to translate ideas into a roadmap with clear definitions of success.
Deliver Mechanical Excellence: Write high-quality user stories and acceptance criteria, ensuring the engineering team understands the "Why" behind every line of code.
Drive Cross-Functional Influence: Work directly with other PMs, Heads, Directors, and Executives to align your product strategy with the wider business vision.
Ensure Operational Integration: Collaborate with operational teams to solve friction points, coordinate go-to-market activities, and manage change for new launches.
Engage in Collaborative Sparring: Participate in high-bandwidth ideation sessions, running ideas through rigorous feedback loops to find the best version of the truth.
Embrace Resource Agility & Team-First Pivots: Manage your backlog with a ruthless eye for value vs. effort, ready to jump into critical company-wide initiatives even if they fall outside your usual territory.
🎯 What You'll Bring

3 to 4 Years of PM Experience: A solid background in Product Management. This is a hands-on role requiring the maturity to operate credibly alongside Heads, Directors, and fellow PMs from day one.
Stakeholder Maturity: The ability to build genuine alignment, hold a well-reasoned 'not yet' while keeping trust intact, and navigate competing priorities with composure.
Systems and Platform Instinct: A deep understanding of how business, data, systems, and platforms interact, especially within "blended" contexts where commercial, operational, and technical realities collide.
Impeccable Product Craft: Demonstrated experience breaking a problem down into PRDs, epics, and well-scoped stories that a squad can pick up without a translation layer.
Holistic Product Judgement & Taste: A visceral reaction to clunky experiences, paired with the ability to turn separate inputs from domain experts into cohesive, effortless product decisions.
Cultural Excellence: A low-ego, high-ownership mindset that thrives on collaborative debate and treats "product health" as a team sport.
💡 What's In It For You

Salary: Earn a competitive salary package per annum plus superannuation
Location: Brisbane City, QLD (less than 10 minute walk to Brisbane Central Station)
Employee Benefits: Paid Parental Leave & Family Subscription Discount
Flexible Hybrid Rhythm: Enjoy the balance of collaborating in our Brisbane office 3 days a week, with the flexibility to work remotely for the rest of the week.
Massive Internal Impact: Own the "products behind the curtain" the vital systems and tools that directly empower our teams and move the needle for the entire business.
High-Trust Culture: Work in a highly collaborative environment that values rigorous feedback, truth-seeking, and leaving egos at the door.
Visibility & Growth: Build deep relationships and win through influence, working daily alongside senior leaders and domain experts across the business.

📕 About The Company

Karmo is Australia’s largest car subscription provider, leading a fast-growing movement toward flexible, affordable, and convenient vehicle access. Our innovative model lets customers regularly swap cars to suit their changing needs offering a smarter alternative to traditional car ownership.

# Cover letter / Application Questions:
```
For the Karmo team,

I'm applying for the Product Manager role with 10 years experience in the product space. Your posting describes owning the products the business runs on, with operators, PMs and Directors living in them every day. That's close to how I've worked, most clearly on self-service purchasing and subscription management at Octopus Deploy, where marketing, sales and support helped shape the work from the start. As well as as planned, pitched and broke down work for the Policies & Compliance feature for Octopus Deploy's Platform Hub enterprise functionality.

Customers leaving a free trial were going back and forth with sales and support to land on the right plan. I ran 5 to 7 discovery interviews across different customers, testing 2 or 3 assumptions in each, and wrote user stories in slices so every release answered a customer opportunity. After kick-off we found the upgrade from the free tier needed more than a price and a button. Customers had to understand what changed between their old and new subscription, pass the compliance checks and meet safety breakpoints before they agreed to buy. I added those needs to the stories, which gave customers a more informed purchase and more control over it. To make room, I made the call to defer the subscription card view revamp in Control Centre to a dedicated Control Centre UI timeline. The Professional tier itself had five resources, each capped at 100 and bought in lots of 10, with one click into a sales conversation above the cap. It ended up working like choosing size, width and colour on a pair of shoes. Starter purchases rose 25% in the first month and 10 customers bought Professional without a sales conversation. The base is small for a specialist enterprise product, but sales spent less time on small accounts and announced enterprise wins more often.

Interviews were hard to get, so I leaned on marketing, sales and support for the voice of the customer. With leadership, sales and marketing I shaped the pricing guidelines and tier comparisons, and with support I tested the flow and wrote support documentation during build and delivery. On Policies and Compliance, operations engineers were losing days finding out why a deployment failed. I wrote the early requirements and defined testing a policy before runtime. A failure now takes hours to correct instead of days.

I write user stories and acceptance criteria with engineers so a squad can pick work up without a translation layer, and I did the same at Amber Labs. I use a RACI and a kick-off canvas so everyone knows their part early, and I'm a certified Scrum Master. At Octopus I ran subscription management, payment gateway improvements, purchase flow optimisation and account portal alignment at the same time, each with an owner and a next step. Eleven years as an insurance broker before design taught me what a renewal, an amendment or a cancellation feels like to the person on the other end.

I'd like to build the tools your teams rely on every day so the customer at the end of the chain gets a better service.

I look forward to hearing from you,
Craig Johnson
