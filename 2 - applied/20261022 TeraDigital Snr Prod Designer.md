Senior Product Designer | Problem Framing, Systems and AI


Customer Engagement Platform
01/The mandate

The most useful work a product designer does often happens before anyone opens Figma. It is asking the questions that expose a missing requirement, making sense of a complicated workflow, or helping a team agree on the problem they actually need to solve.

That is the kind of designer we are looking for.

We are partnering with an established global enterprise SaaS business whose products help large organisations manage customer communications, digital forms, documentation and complex workflows. The business is rolling out a new product strategy, investing in AI-enabled experiences and bringing greater consistency to a portfolio of products.

You will join a small global design team, working closely with a Senior Product Manager and Engineering. There is room to influence product decisions and improve how the team works, but the immediate need is someone who brings structure, clear thinking and sound design judgement to unfamiliar problems.

The role
You will be the primary design partner for a key product area, taking work from early discovery and problem definition through to delivery and iteration. The products involve multiple user types, configuration, permissions, data, approvals and integrations, so understanding the full workflow matters as much as getting an individual screen right.

A request might arrive with a proposed solution but an unclear underlying problem. You will need to work out what is known, what needs testing, whose needs matter and what success should look like. From there, you will develop and communicate a recommendation that Product, Engineering and other stakeholders can work with.

The team wants a designer who can hold a clear view of the experience users need. That means working constructively with engineers on feasibility and trade-offs, while being able to explain when a technical shortcut would create a poor customer experience and why an alternative is worth considering.

You will also help develop a shared design system across the product suite. There is groundwork in place, with opportunities to improve components, patterns, documentation and adoption alongside other designers and front-end engineers. Systems experience is valuable, but so is knowing how to apply it thoughtfully to different products and user needs.

New AI-related product work is part of the roadmap. Your contribution will be to make those experiences understandable and useful, with appropriate controls, feedback and ways to recover when things go wrong. Research, usability testing and iteration will inform the work throughout.

What we are looking for
You will have experience designing complex B2B SaaS, enterprise software or workflow-heavy products. More importantly, you will be able to show a repeatable approach to understanding problems and making decisions, rather than relying on a fully defined brief before you can contribute.

Your portfolio should explain the problem, your personal contribution, the decisions and trade-offs you made, and what happened after delivery. Strong visual craft still matters. We want to see thoughtful interaction design, clear hierarchy, considered typography and attention to detail, supported by an equally clear explanation of your thinking.

You should be comfortable making a recommendation, explaining your reasoning and changing your view when new evidence warrants it. Good collaboration here means contributing a design perspective, not simply collecting stakeholder preferences or waiting for Engineering to determine the experience.

Because the team is distributed, clear written communication is important. Your briefs, flows, annotations and decision notes should help colleagues understand the work and move it forward without needing you to explain everything in a meeting.

Strong Figma skills, research, usability testing, information architecture and prototyping are expected. Experience building or evolving a design system would be valuable, as would accessibility, analytics or a working understanding of front-end development.

We would also like to hear how you use AI in your design practice, what it genuinely improves and where you have found its limits. The judgement you apply to the output matters more than the number of tools you use.

The business remains open to a strong mid-level designer ready for a Senior role, as well as an established Senior who wants to stay hands-on. The level of ownership and discipline you demonstrate matters more than your current title.

How the team works
The role can be based anywhere in Australia, with offices in Sydney, Canberra and Brisbane available if you prefer. You will work with colleagues across Australia, the UK and Portugal.

The working environment is flexible and based on delivery rather than hours spent online. Occasional early-evening calls with European colleagues are part of the role, with flexibility to adjust your day accordingly.

If you enjoy making sense of complex problems, care about the quality of what ships and want a closer partnership with Product and Engineering, apply directly. Please include your portfolio or relevant work samples so we can understand both your craft and your approach.
