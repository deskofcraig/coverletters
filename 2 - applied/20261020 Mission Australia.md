```
Life at Mission Australia

When you join the team at Mission Australia, you become part of a community who are determined to end homelessness and ensure people in need can thrive. United by our Christian purpose and values, we advocate for a fairer Australia, to meet the growing needs of people across the country. Together we’re building hope and possibility for all.

Find out more about us and our purpose.

Your opportunity

Every meaningful service begins with listening, understanding, and a belief that better outcomes are possible. At Mission Australia, we partner with people, communities and colleagues to build hope and create lasting change for those experiencing challenging times.

As our Service Design & Innovation Specialist, you will help shape the future of how we support people across Australia, bringing together lived expertise, human-centred design and evidence-based practice to create services that are more responsive, impactful and sustainable. Working alongside passionate service delivery teams and communities, you'll turn insights into action, helping design innovative approaches that strengthen lives, communities and systems, all while advancing our vision of building a fairer Australia where everyone can thrive.

Your key responsibilities will be to:

Design and develop innovative service models and practice approaches that improve outcomes for people experiencing challenging times.
Partner with service delivery teams, communities and people with lived expertise to co-design services using human-centred design principles.
Translate research, evaluation findings, impact data and best practice evidence into practical service improvements and innovation opportunities.
Support the implementation and continuous improvement of flagship service models, ensuring they remain effective, sustainable and responsive to community needs.
Facilitate workshops, consultation activities and collaborative design processes that engage stakeholders and strengthen participation.
Build organisational capability in co-design and human-centred design through training, coaching, resources and practical support.
Provide expert advice and guidance to leaders and frontline teams on service design, innovation, community development and participation practices.
Develop strong internal and external partnerships to drive strategic initiatives, share insights and support innovation across Mission Australia.
Requirements for success

Tertiary qualifications in a relevant discipline such as Human Services, Social Work, Community Development, Public Policy, Service Design, Strategic Design, Innovation Management or a related field.
Experience in service design, innovation, program development or continuous improvement, ideally within the community, human services or not-for-profit sector.
Demonstrated expertise in human-centred design, co-design methodologies and trauma-informed approaches.
Ability to translate research, evaluation findings and service insights into practical and scalable solutions.
Strong project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver outcomes in complex environments.
Experience facilitating workshops, stakeholder engagement activities and collaborative design processes, both virtually and in person.
Exceptional relationship-building skills, with the ability to influence, collaborate and engage diverse stakeholders.
Strong analytical and critical thinking skills, including the ability to synthesise complex information and identify opportunities for improvement.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to present ideas, develop resources and communicate complex concepts clearly.
Before starting work with us, you will need to undertake a national police history check, qualifications, and referee checks, and have a clearance to work with children/vulnerable people.
```

# Cover letter / Application Questions:
```
For the Mission Australia team,

I’m applying for the Service Design & Innovation Specialist role. Your posting describes turning what people with lived expertise and service delivery teams tell you into services that respond better to people in challenging times, and that’s the work I’ve done on the design side for 10 years, mostly across government, health and enterprise. My portfolio is at deskofcraig.com.

The closest match is my service design work at Cognizant. I led research and co-design for a Law Enforcement portal and worked on Queensland Police service design, where the aim was better operational knowledge sharing inside the agency, between districts and with non-government agencies. I mapped the service end to end across the people, systems and handoffs involved, not only the screens an operator would see, and ran co-design sessions with the people who would use it across 5 sessions involving police, non-government organisations and district councils. During these sessions, I uncovered pain and friction points relating to speed and detail of key information being required by actors during service operations. We audited and tested the result to a standard above WCAG best practice.

Working with people who hold lived expertise started earlier for me. At the University of Queensland’s Design Research Lab, I spent a summer ideating, building and testing tactile interfaces with people living with dementia. At Cognizant I also led the design system for Healius pathology, covering collection technicians, couriers, lab staff and the patients who receive sensitive results, so each group saw a different slice of the same process. For the Central Queensland University 10,000 Steps program, I led the redesign as native iOS and Android apps, and the App Store rating moved from about 1.5 to 3.1.

On evidence, I read behaviour data next to interviews and turn both into opportunities. At CoreLogic (now Cotality Australia) I A/B tested the e-store purchase flow using Hotjar data and customer feedback, and each funnel segment improved 15 to 20% over four weeks. I’ve run workshops in person and on video, and completed the AJ & Smart Workshop Facilitation Masterclass in 2023.

Building capability in other people is something I’ve done for years. I tutored user experience, design thinking and human computer interaction at UQ from 2016 to 2018, and I’ve trained designers at CoreLogic, Cognizant and Octopus Deploy on design thinking, accessibility and documentation standards. I’m a certified Scrum Master and have planned projects up to 12 months out with onshore and offshore engineers.

My degree is in user experience design, not human services, and I haven’t worked inside a not-for-profit. Trauma-informed practice is where I’d lean on your frontline teams first.

Designing services with the people who use them, then changing what the evidence says to change, is the work I’d like to do for the people Mission Australia supports.

I look forward to hearing from you,
Craig Johnson
```
