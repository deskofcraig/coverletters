Human Centered Design Manager

Company OverviewVoted #1 EHR by PC Mag, WRS Health delivers a fully integrated cloud based EMR and practice management solutions to its clients. We bring solutions to physicians by providing constant enhancement of our products and services including EHR, practice management, marketing, patient coordination and billing.Job OverviewWe are seeking a Human-Centered Design (HCD) Manager to bridge the gap between user needs and technical execution. You will lead qualitative research, map user journeys, build interactive prototypes, and run usability tests to ensure our products are intuitive, accessible, and impactful for the physicians, clinical staff, billers, front-desk teams, and patients who rely on them every day. In a clinical setting, a confusing screen costs a provider time, adds to burnout, and can affect patient safety, so this role carries real weight. You will thrive here if you love sitting with users in their actual workflows, can turn messy observations into clear direction, and can defend design decisions with evidence.Key ResponsibilitiesUser Research
Conduct contextual inquiries, interviews, field observations, and surveys with physicians, clinical staff, billers, and patients to uncover explicit and latent needs
Partner with account managers and support teams to surface usability pain points from client feedback and support tickets
Synthesis & Strategy
Translate research into personas, journey maps, service blueprints, and system workflows across clinical, operational, and billing processes
Present insights to product and executive leadership to shape roadmap priorities
Ideation & Prototyping
Facilitate cross-functional design workshops and build low- to high-fidelity wireframes and interactive prototypes in Figma, Sketch, or Adobe XD
Design AI-powered features (AI charting, AI agents) that keep clinicians in control and make AI output easy to review and correct
Usability Testing
Plan, moderate, and analyze formative and summative usability tests to iterate on designs before and during development
Evaluate safety-critical workflows such as medication management and e-prescribing, and support ONC Safety-Enhanced Design testing
Collaboration & Governance
Partner with product managers, business analysts, and engineers in Agile/Scrum teams to maintain design integrity from concept through release
Uphold accessibility standards (WCAG, Section 508) and HIPAA-safe research practices across all products
Leadership
Build and scale WRS Health's HCD practice, including research operations, templates, and standards
Mentor designers and researchers, and coach teammates on human-centered methods
QualificationsRequired
Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Human-Centered Design, Human-Factors Engineering, Interaction Design, Cognitive Psychology, or a related field
Experience: 3 to 6+ years of professional experience in HCD, UX design, or user research, including enterprise or regulated-industry experience
Technical Skills: Proficiency in prototyping software (Figma, Sketch, or Adobe XD), qualitative research methodologies, usability testing frameworks, and Agile/Scrum delivery flows
Accessibility: Working knowledge of Section 508 and WCAG standards and how to apply them in practice
Core Competencies: Strong empathy, verbal storytelling, analytical problem-solving, and the ability to defend design decisions with data
A portfolio or case studies demonstrating end-to-end research, synthesis, prototyping, and measurable impact
Preferred
Experience designing for EHR, practice management, revenue cycle, telehealth, or other health IT products
Familiarity with clinical workflows and terminology across ambulatory specialties
Knowledge of ONC Safety-Enhanced Design (user-centered design and summative testing) requirements, NISTIR 7804, or ISO 9241-210
Experience designing AI-assisted or automation features, including human-in-the-loop review patterns
Prior experience leading or mentoring designers/researchers, or building a design function from the ground up
Experience with remote research and unmoderated testing tools and product analytics platforms
Clinical background or prior work in a medical practice setting
 A Day in the LifeYour days move between the field, the whiteboard, and the prototype. You might start by joining the product team's stand-up to flag a research insight affecting an upcoming sprint, then sit in (virtually or on-site) with a specialty practice to watch how the front desk, medical assistants, and physicians actually work, noting the workarounds they've built and where the screens slow them down. Later, you could be synthesizing those observations into an updated journey map, facilitating a workshop with product managers, engineers, and billing specialists to sketch solutions, or moderating a usability test of a new AI charting feature to see how a clinician reviews and trusts AI-drafted notes. Before the day ends, you refine prototypes in Figma based on what you learned, check designs against accessibility standards, share prioritized recommendations with product leadership, and make time to coach the designers and researchers on your team.Success Metrics
Measurable usability gains: Improved task success rates, time-on-task, error rates, and System Usability Scale (SUS) scores for redesigned workflows, including fewer clicks and less documentation time for key clinical tasks
Research-driven roadmap: Consistent monthly contact with users across specialties, with a clear, visible share of roadmap items originating from or validated by HCD research
Fewer usability issues post-release: Decline in usability-related support tickets, rework, and post-launch design defects
Accessibility and certification compliance: New and updated features meet WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA and Section 508 standards at release, and ONC Safety-Enhanced Design documentation is completed on time
Adoption and satisfaction: Higher feature adoption (including AI features) and improved client satisfaction or NPS for redesigned areas

Compensation will be based on location, experience, and qualifications.This job description outlines the general responsibilities of the role and is not exhaustive. Additional duties may be assigned as needed to support business operations.WRS Health is an equal opportunity employer.
