# Job description:

```
Digital Experience Manager

At Amart, we're more than a furniture retailer. For over 50 years, we've helped Australians create beautiful homes with stylish, affordable furniture – and we're only getting started.

As we continue to invest in our digital experience, we're looking for a Full Time Digital Experience Manager to help lead how customers experience our online store, sitting within our CRM & Ecommerce team.

Working closely with our Online Trading, Development, Email Marketing, Graphic Design and Paid teams, you'll bring our Data 360 and personalisation capability to life through thoughtful, high-performing digital experiences. This is a hands-on opportunity to combine UX/UI, ecommerce and optimisation to deliver for both our customers and our commercial goals.

What you’ll be doing

Own the end-to-end performance of key website journeys - designing, testing, and iterating to lift conversion, engagement and revenue
Translate Amart’s customer segments and data-driven triggers into clear, intentional UI and interaction patterns across the site
Partner with our SEO and content specialists to ensure page structure, metadata and content are optimised for both traditional search and generative/AI search engines (GEO), so our products are easy to discover wherever customers are searching
Use data and analytics from Constructor, Fullstory and GA4 to identify friction, prioritise opportunities, and validate decisions pre- and post-launch
Design end-to-end experiences: user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity UI for Amart’s website, from landing pages for the Paid Media team and first-to-market product launch pages, to re-vamped product pages
Plan, run, and synthesise customer insights and utilise Optimizely to inform design, tests and resulting optimisation decisions
Contribute to and help maintain our design system, building personalisation-ready components that scale
Partner closely with developers to ensure designs are implemented accurately and to a high standard
Present and rationalise your work to stakeholders, connecting design decisions back to commercial outcomes
About you

You'll be someone who's genuinely energised by ecommerce performance and design. You will see a drop in conversion or a search visibility gap as a design problem, not just a marketing one.

You'll also bring:

3 years' experience in a UX/UI, digital product, or CRO/optimisation-focused ecommerce role
Experience using any of the following tools looked upon favourably: Salesforce, Fullstory, Constructor, Optimizely (or equivalent testing/personalisation platforms) and Google Analytics
A working understanding of SEO fundamentals, and curiosity about how generative/AI search is changing how customers discover products online
Strong craft across interaction and visual design, with fluency in tools like Figma
Comfort using qualitative and quantitative data to defend design and optimisation decisions
Strong collaboration skills and the ability to communicate design rationale clearly to non-designers
Why join Amart?

Our culture is genuinely people-first. We back our team with real support. You’ll be joining a team where your experience is valued, your contribution matters and you can make a real impact.

The Amart difference:

Since 1970, Amart has been a trusted name in Australian retail. Work alongside experienced industry professionals in an established business with a reputation you can be proud of
Modern office space in Rochedale with free on-site parking
Work from home flexibility
A supportive, high-energy team that celebrates success together
Exclusive team member discounts - industry-leading savings for you and your family
Club Epic rewards program with exclusive discounts from 500+ retailers
Giving back through our partnership with Ronald McDonald House® Australia
Interested?

If you're a digital and ecommerce specialist who gets excited by UX, conversion, optimisation and commercial performance, we'd love to hear from you.
```
