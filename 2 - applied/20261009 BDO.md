About The Role

We are seeking a UX & Content Designer to join BDO's Information Technology team on a 2-year max-term contract, helping shape and enhance the experience of Global Portal, BDO's client collaboration platform.

Reporting to the Global Portal Product Manager, you will combine user research, experience design and content design expertise to understand user needs, identify opportunities for improvement and influence the evolution of the platform. Working closely with technology, product and business stakeholders, you'll help create intuitive, accessible and effective digital experiences that improve how BDO teams and clients collaborate.

You'll join a collaborative team focused on innovation, user experience and continuous improvement, working closely with stakeholders across Australia and the broader global network.

Enhance information architecture to improve usability and content discoverability.
Partner with product, technology and business stakeholders to deliver user-centred solutions.

Hello to the BDO hiring team,

I’m applying for the UX & Content Designer role with 10+ years experience as a product and experience designer. I have experience across enterprise SaaS, consumer mobile applications, service design, e-commerce, in-house, and consultancy. 

Global Portal serves two groups doing the same work, BDO staff managing engagements and clients handing over documents and answering requests, and I’ve spent most of the last decade designing for that kind of split. At Cognizant I led the design system for Healius pathology, covering collection technicians, couriers, lab testing and patients. I also led the research and co-design for a Law Enforcement portal. Each audience needed a different view of the same process, and the design had to hold together for all of them.

I run my own research. At Octopus Deploy I organised and facilitated the customer interviews and workshops for self-service purchasing and subscription management, then used Amplitude and Fullstory to see where people stalled. We tested the a la carte selection for the Professional tier with marketing, sales and a closed group of users, and landed on a pattern like buying a pair of shoes online, because everyone already knows how that works. Starter tier purchases rose 25% in the first month and 10 customers bought Professional on their own. Those numbers look small, but it’s a niche DevOps product sold mostly to enterprises, and sales started announcing enterprise wins more often because they spent less time on small accounts.

Client-facing platforms are familiar ground. At CoreLogic (now Cotality Australia) I owned UX for the billing portal, which won a 2021 Pinnacle Award, and I designed a single sign-on experience across the whole product suite. I analysed customer feedback and session recordings on purchasing and onboarding, and after the sign-on launched, support tickets about multiple passwords and accounts fell steadily.

The content side of your posting is work I do myself rather than hand to someone else. At CoreLogic and Octopus I wrote and implemented the copy for onboarding, purchasing, product comparisons and activation. At Octopus that included the pricing and tier comparison guides I built with marketing, which is where the Starter tier lift came from, so the differences between three tiers were clear at a glance. On information architecture, I realigned the drill-down page layouts and the placement of assets on level 1 and 2 pages as the product grew. I designed the test status and result tags in Policies so an operations engineer can read a result without digging for it, and I wrote the usage guidance in Supernova so engineers weren’t guessing. I’ve also used SharePoint at Cognizant and built assets in it.

On accessibility, the Queensland Police portal was audited and tested to a standard above WCAG best practice, and I’ve held to AA in everything since. I have a Bachelor of Information Technology in User Experience Design from UQ, I work in Figma every day, and I’ve presented recommendations to senior leaders at Cognizant and up to the CEO at Octopus. I’m comfortable explaining design decisions to a product manager and to business stakeholders in the same meeting. The 2-year contract term suits me.

My portfolio is at deskofcraig.com and I look forward to hearing from you,

Regards,
Craig Johnson
