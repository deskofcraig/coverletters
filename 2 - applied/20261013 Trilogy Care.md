Senior Product Designer
at Trilogy Care Pty Ltd.
Digital – Product Development • Bowen Hills, Queensland 4006, Australia • Full-time

Role Type
On-site • Permanent • Full-time • Associate
Description
At Trilogy Care, we’re not polishing an old model. We’re rebuilding aged care from the ground up with AI, automation, and cloud-native tech. As an industry first-mover in practical AI, we’ve put autonomy, experimentation, and pace at the centre of our tech team. From development to front-line care, everyone has generative AI at their fingertips. 

You’ll join a fast-growing product design and engineering function in Brisbane, working with engineers, data specialists, and care coordinators. At this level, you won’t just deliver great work. You’ll lead complex initiatives, shape product direction, and lift the whole team through standards, coaching, and influence. 

The role 
We’re looking for a Senior Product Designer to lead design on complex, cross-cutting initiatives. The work spans the Trilogy Care Portal, our flagship internal care coordination and billing platform, and our consumer experiences, including an ecommerce marketplace and React Native mobile apps. 

You’ll operate with high autonomy, define problems as well as solutions, and partner with leadership on product strategy and roadmap. Success is measured by the team’s output, not just your own. 

This is a full-time hybrid role: three days in our Bowen Hills office, two from home. 

What you’ll do 
Lead the work 

Lead complex, ambiguous initiatives that span teams and domains, from discovery through to production 
Define the problem as well as the solution: shape what we build, not just how it looks 
Make clear trade-offs between user needs, business goals, and technical constraints 
Represent design in prioritisation, planning, and roadmap conversations with leadership 
Design at the level of systems 

Set the craft bar: your work becomes the reference for others 
Design patterns, not just screens, and evolve the design system as you go 
Spot systemic issues and fix them at the root, so the same problem doesn’t come back 
Close the design-to-code gap 

Ship code directly for appropriate work using AI-assisted tools like Claude Code 
Review on working code, not just static mockups, and catch issues where they matter 
Multiply the team 

Coach and grow other designers through critique, pairing, and mentorship 
Lead design critiques and raise the quality of feedback 
Shape how design operates: process, standards, and tooling 
Step up to lead the design function when needed 
What you’ll bring 
5+ years in product design, with clear senior-level impact 
A portfolio that shows leadership on complex, ambiguous work: how you framed the problem, influenced direction, and made the team better, not just the final screens 
Strong craft across usability, interaction, and visual design, plus systems thinking that turns one-off fixes into reusable patterns 
Experience with complex B2B or internal platforms, with consumer or ecommerce experience a real bonus 
Hands-on fluency with modern tools (Figma, Storybook, Claude, v0, Vercel) and experience shipping code, or a strong drive to learn AI-assisted implementation 
A track record of lifting a team through coaching, standards, or process 
Confident communication with executives, PMs, engineers, SMEs, and users, in groups and 1:1 
Comfort with uncertainty, and the ability to lead others through it 
Commercial awareness: you know when to push on craft and when to move fast 
A real commitment to designing products that improve people’s lives 
Why Trilogy Care 
Lead meaningful work that helps older Australians live at home for longer, better, and in more comfort 
Shape how a growing design team operates, not just what it makes 
Work where design meets code, with AI-assisted workflows as standard 
Partner directly with leadership on product strategy

How to apply 
If you’re ready to lead complex work, lift a team, and shape the future of aged care, we’d love to hear from you. We’re not saying we do product perfectly yet, but Trilogy is a damn good place to get there. 

Please include a portfolio (website or PDF) or a Loom walking through a project or two. Show us how you approached an ambiguous problem, influenced direction, and made the people around you better. 

Please note: applicants must have full Australian work rights. A police check and professional references are required during onboarding. 

Company Overview
Trilogy Care is one of Australia’s fastest-growing providers of home care. We support older Australians to live independently by offering a flexible, self-managed approach to care. Our model gives clients more choice, control and value, allowing them to spend less on fees and more on the support that matters most. Join a purpose-driven team that’s transforming home care through innovation and client empowerment.
