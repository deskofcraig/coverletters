```
Senior UI/UX Designer

https://simpro.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/en-GB/Simpro_External_Careers/job/Senior-UI-UX-Designer_JR102224

About the job
Job

Simpro is trusted by thousands of field service businesses worldwide to run their operations. As our Senior UX Designer, you won't just ship screens - you'll raise the design bar across the entire product organisation, setting the standards that everyone else builds from.

Design authority, not just delivery. The AI era means more people than ever can generate UI. Your job is to ensure what gets built is intentional, cohesive, and genuinely good - through systems, standards, and the credibility to enforce them.

What You’ll Do


Own and evolve Simpro's design system and component library, setting the standards that product managers and engineers use when they build UI themselves
Define UX guidelines, principles, and governance frameworks so that design quality scales beyond the design team
Lead user research initiatives - regular usability testing, discovery interviews, and insight synthesis - and turn findings into product direction
Partner with product and engineering on complex, cross-product features: facilitating trade-off conversations and driving toward agreed solutions without losing user advocacy
Prototype and validate new concepts fast, keeping pace with AI-accelerated development cycles
Coach and mentor designers on best practices and evolve in the AI age
Take concepts from wireframe through to polished, production-ready design — and into code when needed


What You’ll Bring


5+ years of UX/UI design experience, with meaningful time in B2B, B2C or SaaS
A strong portfolio demonstrating systems thinking, not just polished screens
Hands-on experience with AI design and prototyping tools (v0, Cursor, Figma AI, Claude, or similar)
Confidence running your own research: recruiting, facilitation, synthesis
Solid HTML, CSS/SASS, and JavaScript — you can talk code with engineers and build when you need to
Outstanding written and verbal communication
```
