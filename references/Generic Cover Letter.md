Hello to the hiring team,

I’m a senior product and experience designer with 10+ years across enterprise SaaS, fintech, native mobile and e-commerce, and I work the whole way through, from the first customer interview to the build review. Most of my work takes something that needs a sales call or a support ticket and turns it into something the customer can finish alone. My portfolio is at deskofcraig.com, with case studies behind everything below.

At Octopus Deploy I led two joint outcomes over two years. The first was self-service purchasing and subscription management for the Starter and Professional tiers, where customers had been going back and forth with sales and technical support to land on the right plan. I ran the customer interviews and workshops myself, then tested the Professional selection (five resources, capped at 100, bought in lots of 10, one click to sales above the cap) in stages with marketing, sales and a closed user group. It ended up working like choosing size, width and colour on a pair of shoes. Starter purchases rose 25% in the first month and 10 customers bought Professional without a sales conversation. The base is small because it’s a niche DevOps product, but sales spent less time on small accounts and announced enterprise wins more often. The second outcome was Policies and Compliance, where pre-runtime testing catches a broken deployment before it runs, so operations teams fix a failure in hours instead of days.

Other roles show the range. At Cognizant I led the redesign of the CQU 10,000 Steps app as native iOS and Android apps, and the App Store rating went from about 1.5 to 3.1. I also led the design system for Healius pathology, covering technicians, couriers, lab testing and patients, and the research and co-design for a Law Enforcement portal. At CoreLogic (now Cotality Australia) I owned UX for the billing portal, which won a 2021 Pinnacle Award, and A/B tested the e-store purchase flow, with each funnel segment improving 15 to 20% over four weeks. At Amber Labs I designed buying, selling, sending and funding for a bitcoin onramp that was nominated for a Finnies Award.

On the product side, I write requirements and user stories, map scenarios and journeys, slice work into releases, and use a RACI and a kick-off canvas so everyone knows their part before work starts. I’ve built or managed five design systems, most recently with the Foundations team at Octopus across Figma, Supernova and Storybook, and I’ve built React components alongside engineers. Everything I hand over is held to WCAG AA. I’ve guided junior and senior designers and run internal training at Cognizant, CoreLogic and Octopus.

Claude and Figma MCP tooling sit in my daily workflow for clustering interview insights, drafting flows and prototyping, and I built product-helper, an open-source suite of Claude skills (github.com/deskofcraig/product-helper). AI speeds up the mechanics, and understanding the problem stays with me and the team.

Before design I spent 11 years as an insurance broker, so I know what a customer is dealing with when a renewal, a claim or a payment goes wrong. I’ve worked remotely for 3 years, in companies from 8 people to global.

I’d like to bring that to a team that wants its customers to finish the job without a phone call.

I look forward to hearing from you,
Craig Johnson
