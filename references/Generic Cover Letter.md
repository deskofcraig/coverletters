Hello the to hiring team 👋,

I’m a senior product and experience designer with 10+ years across enterprise SaaS, fintech, native mobile and e-commerce. The thread through most of it is taking something that needs a person in the loop, a sales call or a support ticket, and building it so the customer can finish it alone. My portfolio is at deskofcraig.com, with case studies on the self-service work below.

At Octopus Deploy I led two joint outcomes over two years. The first was self-service purchasing and subscription management for the Starter and Professional tiers, where customers had been going back and forth with sales reps and technical support just to land on the right plan. I ran the customer interviews and workshops myself, then tested the a la carte Professional selection (five resources, capped at 100, bought in lots of 10, one click to sales above the cap) with marketing, sales and a closed user group. I also reworked the tier comparison charts and the free trial to paid upgrade flow with marketing, and used Amplitude and Fullstory to find where people stalled. Starter purchases rose 25% in the first month and 10 customers bought Professional without a sales conversation. It’s a niche DevOps product so the base is small, but sales spent less time on small accounts and started announcing enterprise wins more often.

The second outcome was Policies and Compliance for the enterprise tier. I led discovery, design and delivery with product leads and engineers, including pre-runtime compliance testing that catches a deployment that would break before it runs. Operations teams used to spend days finding the cause of a failure and now correct it in hours.

Earlier work backs this up. At CoreLogic (now Cotality Australia) I owned UX for the billing portal, which won a 2021 Pinnacle Award, and A/B tested the e-store purchase flow from Hotjar data, with each funnel segment improving 15 to 20% over four weeks. At Cognizant I led the redesign of the CQU 10,000 Steps app as native iOS and Android apps, and the App Store rating went from about 1.5 to 3.1. I’ve built or managed five design systems, most recently with the Foundations team at Octopus across Figma, Supernova and Storybook, where I wrote the usage guidance and state rules so engineers weren’t guessing. Everything I hand over is held to WCAG AA.

Before design I spent 11 years as an insurance broker, so I know what a customer is dealing with when a renewal, a claim or a payment goes wrong.

I’d like to do this work for a team whose customers should be able to get things done without picking up the phone.

I look forward to hearing from you,
Craig Johnson
