Hello to the Atlassian team 👋

I am excited to be applying for a Product Designer role at Atlassian, a true global leader and Australia success story. I have been using Atlassian products for a decade now and understand from a user, and a designer of enterprise products point of view just how complex they can be. There is a definite need for strong user experience foundations in enterprise products, that can help its community of users and customers achieve their goals successfully and without friction. A good design system can help the product succeed, a great design system can raise the bar for what other products want to be. I am extremely passionate about design systems and component libraries and their role in successful products and experiences. They help product teams deliver work that is consistent to the rest of a product and suite, as well as helps its users trust and have confidence in the product they are using. 

I have 10+ years experience in user experience and product design. I have knowledge and experience across enterprise SaaS, consumer applications, websites, B2B, B2C, in-house and consulting. In each of these roles  I have led the creation of design systems and component libraries from marketing needs to health organisation sample and results management. When building out the design systems, I keep in mind “what is the intent of this asset?” and “where in a journey will this asset appear?”. Other questions when pitching new components that I ask are “does the asset make sense?”, “how is the asset to be used?”, and “how does the asset help a user complete their job to be done?”. These are important questions to me when building out and adding assets to the design system and appropriate supporting documentation. These questions also are important when considering interaction patterns and making sure best practices for accessible user interfaces are maintained. 

Whilst at Corelogic, I introduced the concept of design systems and set up the initial necessary assets for the Marketing team to help with the company’s Australian website revamp that was driven by measuring and testing user journeys through analytics. Whilst at Cognizant, I helped lead the design system and component library for our client Healius when they were overhauling and redeveloping their pathology collection, tracking, and records systems. This work covered assets for interfaces for collection technicians, couriers, lab testing, and patients as well as understanding the journeys and roles each actor has in the process. Whilst at Octopus Deploy, I would regularly contribute to the organisational design system and component library. This was done through communication and working closely with the Foundations team, and a process of critique, discovery, and requirement to make sure a new asset is of value to the product teams. Recently, I updated and realigned the drill down page layouts and hierarchy of asset placement on a level 1 and 2 pages making the layouts more aligned with the growing product interface needs. Outside of employment I have set up token libraries for Figma based on popular frameworks and SDKs foundational colour schemes. These are available via Figma Community. 

As a product designer, I have worked with multi-disciplinary teams throughout my career. These teams have involved designers, engineers, product managers, and at times stakeholders. I have helped these teams turn ambiguity into scalable solutions through continuous discovery. Through this I have turned opportunities into problem statements, and assumptions into validation testing. This has made a path for clear recommendations and requirements that account for edge cases, accessibility, and implementation constraints. When working on design system assets, I have partnered with designers, engineers, and product managers to make sure components, assets, and interaction patterns meet the standards set by the company, what the users should expect, and that they are documented to a high standard as well as reusable throughout the rest of the product and suite. 

Recently I was employed at Octopus Deploy, a highly technical enterprise SaaS product as part of their Platform Hub team. During my work on Policies Testing and Evaluations, the design system did not have considerations for what our interfaces needed. The testing and evaluations data being returned required some specific methods of interaction and feedback that had not been available previously, with no current design system options offering a passable solution. I documented the needs and asset concepts for a component discovery request with the Foundations team. After reviewing and discovery, it was decided that new components should be made available to meet the changing needs of the customers, the product, and the business. I then lead the development of the new assets working with front-end engineers to develop, code, and document the new component and its variants in Figma, Supernova and Storybook through branching and merging once reviewed so it could be used throughout the rest of the business. During this time, I developed an agentic AI product team consisting of design, engineer, and product owner roles at different seniorities, skillsets, and processes. I created this to not handover delegation or understanding, but to bolster what my product team was already doing. Once set up, turn around times become more efficient, and it assisted in optimising workflows and processes, helping our team to desirable outcomes sooner. 

I have recently joined the Friends of Figma Brisbane group as a committee member, recognised for my knowledge and experimental inquisitiveness with Figma.

I look forward to discussing this opportunity further with you. I believe my experiences, knowledge, and passion for Design Systems would make a great addition to the Design Team at Atlassian.
