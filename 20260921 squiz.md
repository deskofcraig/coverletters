Hello to the Squiz recruitment team 👋

As a tagline, I help companies reach design milestones & outcome driven innovation for their products & their community. I am excited to apply to Squiz as a Senior Product Designer.

I am passionate about creating and developing interfaces and platforms that make sense. Not only do they have to make sense, they have to stand the test of time. To do this I make sure concepts and assumptions are validated. This will help drive outcomes into the correct direction where they are desirable, feasible, usable, and viable. When designing interfaces, remembering that a human is needing to use this space is of utmost importance. “What is the job they are doing?”, “what are they looking to achieve?, “is the information they require visible or discoverable at the appropriate time?”. These important questions get asked consistently to make sure that the work being done gets the right direction, and moves towards the ideal outcome. 

I am experienced with working in collaboration with multi-discipline Product Teams involving designers, engineers and managers, with at times more senior members and stakeholders. When I work in these teams, I use them for helping strategise current and upcoming work, look for the opportunities to maybe pursue, what data could we use to help make decisions about features. My work in these teams also helps to balance the feasibility of work and understand “what can we do now” as low hanging fruit, and needing to create a roadmap to get to the solution if integrations or resources are not yet available. My involvement in product teams has led to multiple successful deliveries efficiently with having to consider trade-off, and viability without having to lower the quality of the product or feature. When I work with a product team from an early stage, pitching, briefs, timelines, and handovers are easily agreed upon because we have all been understanding the problems and opportunities together. 

Stakeholders and customers are always the key to successful deliveries. I always maintain strong communication with stakeholders and make sure they are able to follow and be updated on the direction and journey a feature or product is taking. This can be looking for input, deeper context, or just letting them know where the work is at. This can be useful when done through constant storytelling via user journey mapping, scenarios, mental modelling.

I have 10+ years in product and user experience design, from B2B and B2C, from consumer apps to highly technical enterprise SaaS, and from in-house to consultancy. I have led design, built and managed Design Systems from the ground and documented for wider use. I have implemented AI work flows to help disseminate and drive processes forward, whilst not relying on AI to understand the work at hand for me. I have led design and design strategy for many projects to help deliver outcomes that stakeholders and customers require. I am experienced with customer interviews and testing (in both discovery and design phases) to ensure the features and projects meet customer expectations and mental models. All of these skills and strengths together are grouped and bound together with a strong framework of human centred interaction and experiences.
