UX Designer | Australia | Fully Remote 
We’re looking for a UX Designer who loves turning complex problems into simple, intuitive experiences and who understands how great design translates into great products.
You’ll own the end-to-end design process, from user research and wireframing through to high-fidelity prototypes and design systems, working closely with Product and Engineering to build and evolve fintech products.
A key part of this role is working within a React/TypeScript environment. You don’t need to be a developer, but you should understand how React components, states and reusable design patterns influence what can be built and scaled.
What You’ll Do
• Designing intuitive experiences for complex financial products and workflows• Creating user flows, wireframes and high-fidelity interactive prototypes• Evolving and maintaining a scalable design system aligned with our React component library• Partnering closely with Product Managers and Engineers from concept through to deployment• Conducting user research and usability testing to validate and refine designs
What we’re looking for:
• 3+ years’ experience in UX/UI or Product Design• Strong Figma skills, including auto-layout, variables and interactive components• A practical understanding of React and component-based design• Experience simplifying complex workflows and information• Strong communication and stakeholder management skills• Fintech, banking, SaaS or enterprise software experience is highly regarded
Must haves:• Experience working with React Developers • Full working rights 
If you’re a UX Designer who enjoys solving complex problems, collaborating closely with engineers and creating products that are both beautiful and buildable, I’d love to hear from you.

Requirements added by the job poster

• 2+ years of work experience with Enterprise Software

• 2+ years of work experience with Software as a Service (SaaS)
