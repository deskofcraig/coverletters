Mobile App Product Designer
Budget Direct

Design new App features and define and manage the Design Systems for our mobile app, critical to creating Australia's best mobile insurance experience. 
The Role 
Be part of a team focused on developing a market leading mobile App” for A&G customers. You will be part of a small dedicated team with the end-to-end responsibility for the successful execution of the mobile strategy.
Working with the Product Owner, you will be responsible for the defining and setting up a Design Systems for the Mobile App capability. You will need to take and work with the existing UI/UX standards, guidelines and principles and adapt them to mobile app designs. You will then work with the team to complete the UX andUI design for the features and functionality being deployed into the mobile app. You’ll be a part of a significant strategy to create and maintain Australia’s best mobile insurance experience.
Location: Brisbane (Toowong)

Position Accountabilities
Lead the design approach for a Mobile App development project.
Define, Implement and maintain the Design System that will provide the base capability for the Mobile App into the future.
Working with the existing wider UI/UX team to adapt the current UI/UX Standards and Guidelines to meet the needs / requirements of a Mobile App Solution.
Collaborate with the Mobile App team and wider business stakeholders to discover and ideate on the features and experiences to be deployed in the App.
Collaborate with Business Analysts and greater stakeholders to gather and understand business requirements.
Design and validate your ideas, with engagement across business and customer stakeholders (from a mix of low to high fidelity wireframes and prototypes).
Produce concept wireframes through to finalised detailed designs with all the relevant experience and interactive specs to support implementation.
Collaborate closely with your app development team to ensure that the designed solution is able to be successfully developed and deployed.
Apply UX/UI best practices across your work, particularly covering native app functionality.
Using qualitative and quantitative research insights to inform direction whilst collaborating with business stakeholders to determine Desirability, Viability, Feasibility.

Proficiency in using design tools, with a strong preference for  Figma. The ability to present your ideas and designs clearly and effectively to the business.
A good understanding of design methodologies, best practice usability and standards.
Experience with usability testing and customer research activities.
Prior experience working in Agile methodologies, and leveraging tools such as Atlassian Confluence and Jira, or equivalent. 
Desirable:
A demonstrated experience of being the lead designer for customer facing projects.
Experience in designing for multiple devices and operating systems.
Experience working in, or knowledge of finance, insurance products preferred
Experience in using qualitative and quantitative data or insights to identify improvement opportunities.
Experience in A/B testing and CRO initiatives to optimise digital experiences.
