Senior UI/UX App Designer
Virtual CTO - https://thevirtualcto.com.au/

About the role

Virtual CTO is seeking an experienced Senior UI/UX App Designer to join our growing team on a contract basis. This is an exciting opportunity to shape the digital experiences for innovative applications in a fast-paced environment. Based in Sydney NSW 2000, this role offers the chance to work with cutting-edge design principles and modern design tools. You will take a strategic approach to user interface and user experience design, ensuring that every interaction is intuitive, engaging and aligned with business objectives. As a senior designer, you will play a pivotal role in establishing design standards and driving the creative vision of our digital products.

Key responsibilities
Design and develop user interfaces and user experiences for mobile and web applications, ensuring consistency with brand guidelines and design systems
Conduct user research and usability testing to inform design decisions and validate design concepts
Create wireframes, prototypes and high-fidelity mockups using industry-standard design tools
Collaborate closely with product managers, developers and stakeholders to translate requirements into compelling design solutions
Establish and maintain design patterns, component libraries and design documentation for ongoing use across projects
Provide design leadership and mentorship to junior designers, fostering a culture of continuous improvement and innovation
Participate in design reviews and present design rationale to cross-functional teams
Stay current with emerging design trends, technologies and best practices in UI/UX design
Optimise user journeys and interface interactions to enhance user satisfaction and product engagement

What we're looking for
4+ years of professional experience in UI/UX design or interaction design, with a portfolio demonstrating expertise in app design
Proficiency in design tools such as Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch or similar industry-standard software
Strong understanding of user-centred design principles, design thinking and usability best practices
Proven experience with user research methodologies, wireframing, prototyping and user testing
Excellent visual design skills with a keen eye for typography, colour theory, layout and visual hierarchy
Demonstrated ability to work collaboratively in multidisciplinary teams and communicate design concepts effectively
Experience with design systems, component libraries and design documentation
Knowledge of responsive and adaptive design principles for multiple platforms and devices
Strong problem-solving abilities and the capacity to balance user needs with business objectives
Familiarity with accessibility standards (WCAG) and inclusive design practices is desirable
Experience with agile development methodologies is highly valued

What we offer

At Virtual CTO, we are committed to supporting our team members with a range of benefits and opportunities. We offer flexible working arrangements that enable you to maintain a healthy work-life balance. As a contract position, you will have the autonomy to manage your own schedule whilst delivering exceptional design work. Our collaborative culture encourages creativity and innovation, and you will have the opportunity to work on diverse, high-impact projects with talented professionals. We also offer competitive compensation and the chance to contribute to products that make a real difference in the digital landscape.

About us

Virtual CTO is a forward-thinking technology company dedicated to delivering innovative digital solutions and strategic technology leadership. We partner with organisations to build exceptional digital experiences and drive technological transformation. Our team is composed of talented designers and developers who are passionate about creating products that solve real user problems. We value collaboration, creativity and a commitment to excellence. At Virtual CTO, we foster an inclusive workplace where diverse perspectives are celebrated and every team member has the opportunity to grow and succeed.

If you are a talented UI/UX designer looking for an exciting contract opportunity to make an impact, we would love to hear from you. Please submit your CV, a link to your portfolio and a cover letter outlining your relevant experience and design philosophy. Apply today and join our creative team at Virtual CTO.
